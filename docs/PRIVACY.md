# Inner Sleeves — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Inner Sleeves ("we", "our", or "the application") handles information when operating the Inner Sleeves YouTube channel and its automated video-publishing software.

## 1. Information we collect

Inner Sleeves does not provide user accounts, registration, or interactive services requiring visitors to submit personal information.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from viewers, track individual viewing activity, or maintain a database of viewers.

## 2. Google and YouTube account information

The application uses the YouTube Data API v3 to publish videos and set custom thumbnails on the operator's YouTube channel.

To do this, the channel operator grants the application OAuth access through Google.

The application uses the following permissions:

- `youtube.upload` — to upload videos.
- `youtube.force-ssl` — to perform authorized YouTube API operations, including setting custom video thumbnails.

We do not sell, rent, or use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes. We do not use the YouTube API to collect viewer information, comments, or viewing histories.

The application does not retain a separate database of YouTube account information.

The channel operator can revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

## 3. Third-party services

The application relies on the following services to generate videos:

- **Last.fm:** Used to discover albums and retrieve album artwork.
- **Deezer:** Used to retrieve audio previews associated with selected albums.
- **Google / YouTube:** Used to authenticate the channel operator and upload videos and thumbnails.
- **GitHub Actions:** Used to execute the automation and securely provide configured credentials.

These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not control the data practices of these third parties.

## 4. Data storage and retention

Album artwork, audio previews, and generated videos are downloaded or created temporarily during the execution of the application.

The application does not maintain a permanent database of viewer information.

YouTube OAuth refresh credentials are retained in the configured environment or GitHub repository secrets for as long as the publishing automation requires them.

GitHub and Google may retain information according to their respective policies.

## 5. Cookies and analytics

The Inner Sleeves application does not itself use cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics to track viewers.

YouTube may use its own cookies, analytics, and other technologies when visitors access its platform. Those practices are governed by YouTube's privacy policy.

## 6. Children's privacy

Inner Sleeves is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same URL.
